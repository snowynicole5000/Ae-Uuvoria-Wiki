Oa'Veyra  ·  Western continent

The Glass City

A Makk'ar colony that grew into a massive trading hub on the south coast, between the Fulgari desert to the north and the Isikitem lands to the west. Here, appearance is everything: fashion, jewelry, pets and even building materials all show what class you are.

The sea

Lies along the whole south side of the city. The docks of the Quay and Seaglass face it.

The river

Comes down from the north-east mountains and splits the city in two. The east bank is the poorer side.

The bridges

Where the class line shows most. The Garrison has been keeping checkpoints on them recently.

The wall

Arcs around the north side of the city, facing the desert. Every road through it has a gate.

The rail yard

West of the city, past the Lensworks. Facilitates trade between the city and northern Isikitem lands.

Who runs it

The council meets in the Spire. Most seats go to young heirs of the Makk'ar ruling families, sent here to prove themselves to their families, so they're ambitious, reckless, and always competing. The advisor can veto anything big, so a tantrum from one of them never does too much damage.

The Garrison

The Varrhaku keep order, with checkpoints on the bridges. They patrol The Cullet more than anywhere, and rarely go into the Sink.

The underground

A thriving underground economy keeps the bottom of the city running. The council pretends it doesn't exist.

The Sink

A huge sinkhole in the east of the city, between the Kilns, Seaglass and The Cullet. Long ago it was an ancient printworks, built on the deposit the Au'Uuvori quarried to make their building material. The quarrying left huge caverns underneath, and they collapsed.

The Rim

  • Shacks and terraces built into the walls, joined by rope bridges and ladders.
  • Mostly poor Fulgari and Isikitem families.
  • The Garrison rarely patrols here.
2

The Undermarket

  • A black market in old ancient halls partway down. No taxes are ever enforced here.
  • Lit by sunlight and moonlight bounced down shafts with Fulgari mirrors and lenses, so it more or less closes on new-moon nights. That's when the shadiest business happens.
  • Delvers selling ruin maps, contraband, and Insurgent recruiters.
3

The Deep

  • Ancient ruins, with line script still faintly glowing on the walls.
  • The Foundation has a fenced dig site at the top, but the real deep ruins can only be reached through the tunnels.
  • Parts of the old printworks froze mid-print when magic shut off. Half-finished structures still stick out of the printers.
  • Dangerous: collapses, and creatures living in the caves. People who venture down this deep unprepared tend to get trapped.
⬡

At the very bottom

  • A sealed chamber holding the ancient who became the avatar of Order. Its door is printed from the ancient material.

The tunnels

Perfectly hexagonal tunnels branch off every level, with no tool marks and no melting, as if the stone was simply removed from existence. Delvers navigate by scratch marks, which the Foundation hates.

Hidden hatches

Many places east of the river have hidden hatches down into the tunnels, including Foreer's shop in Seaglass.

The ancient material

Stone-like and nearly indestructible. Printers took a little feedstock and used Nature and Order nodes to expand, arrange and solidify it, like spray foam.
