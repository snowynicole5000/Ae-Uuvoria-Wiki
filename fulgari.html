Fulgari

Eight-legged glassworkers of the western desert
Homeland
The western desert
Height
120–130 cm
Lifespan
~80 Earth years
Magic
Solar refraction
Overview

The Fulgari are eight-legged desert burrowers from the western continent of Oa'Veyra, living among huge spikes of fulgurite glass. They are the finest glassworkers on the planet, and they worship the sun.

Their lenses are prized by the Makk'ar, who once enslaved them and still rule over them. Many Fulgari now live and work in the poorest parts of the Glass City, and a rebellion is growing.

Anatomy

Body

Eight legs. The front pair are hands, and the back two pairs are for walking. The pair in between does both: on long walks they stand on six legs, but when working with their hands or carrying something, only two pairs touch the ground.

Hands

The front pair has six fingers on each hand, with four segments per finger. The back pairs have six digits with three joints each.

Size

120–130 cm.

Shell

Chitin in sand tones, with a faint glassy sheen.

Senses

They feel vibrations through ground and sand. Their eyesight is weak.

Voice

They breathe through spiracles along the body and speak by pushing air through several at once, so every word is a chord.

Society & culture

Together

Very collectivist. The group comes before the individual.

The sun

The Fulgari worship the sun.

Solar refraction

Lenses focus sunlight into beams hot enough to melt glass, and can amplify light magic.

Magic today →

Glass

Glass art, lenses and jewelry. Their hand dexterity grew alongside their toolmaking and glasswork.

History
Long ago

Desert burrowers

The Fulgari evolve to burrow in the western desert.

Before the Glass City

A lost cousin

A species related to the Fulgari lives alongside them, until the Makk'ar wipe it out.

Under the Makk'ar

Enslaved

The Makk'ar enslave the Fulgari. They are no longer enslaved, but they are still oppressed and exploited.

Today · 5 KKM

Unrest

A rebellion is growing, and the Makk'ar are tightening their grip to stamp it out.

In the Glass City

Fulgari labor keeps the Glass City running. They have one seat on its council.

Cullet

Poor homes built from broken and remelted scraps of glass, metal and sandstone. Mostly Fulgari, and the Garrison patrols here more than anywhere.

The Kilns

Hot, smoky glassworks downstream by the docks, worked mostly by Fulgari.

The Fringe

Slums outside the wall on the desert side, grown up around a gate. The rich call it Slag.

The Rim

Shacks and terraces built into the walls of the Sink, home to poor Fulgari and Isikitem families.

Groups

Three groups have formed around the Fulgari cause in the Glass City.

The Insurgents

“War will free us.” They want the Makk'ar out and the Glass City for the Fulgari alone. Anyone working with the government is evil, and if they die, it only helps the cause.

The Objectors

“Words before war.” They hate the Makk'ar government and believe an uprising is needed, but want to hurt as few innocent people as possible.

The Reformists

Also called the sympathizers. Mostly performative: they want credit for caring without the messy work that might cost them their status. Some follow them simply because they don't know how bad things are.
