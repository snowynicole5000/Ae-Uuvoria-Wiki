Overview

The Fire clan (their real name is still to come) are bipedal vulture-dragons from the central continent of Oa'Veyra, where a rainforest is slowly burning into wasteland. The volcano at the heart of the continent marks their territory.

They don't fly. Their wings are for fire: fanning flames to drive prey out into the open. The delvers' black market was founded on their land, and a small cult among them worships an eternal flame.

Anatomy
