Biography

Eo'Sevoria is the last living Au'Uuvori, the ancients who lived through the Era of Magic and very nearly all died out after it.

The Librarian lives at the peak of the tallest mountain on Oa'Veyra, the one place in the world that rises above the barrier. From there, Eo'Sevoria keeps the only remaining contact with Ae'Uuvoria, the creator who turned away from the planet after the Era of Darkness.

Ey where a cloak with the night sky embroidered on it in gold with the lines of the constellations in uv reactive thread.

The prefix Eo' means “last": lowest in rank, last in time/place, eldest in family, and, in numbers, infinity.
