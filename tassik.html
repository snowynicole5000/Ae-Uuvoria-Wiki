Overview

The Tassik are low, lean reptiles from the deep canyons of the eastern continent on Oa'Veyra. They crawl along the canyon walls, glide across the gaps, and live in homes cut into the cliffs.

In a Tassik town, height is everything. The higher you live, the more prestigious you are, and the oldest families live closest to the top.

Anatomy

Body

Low and lean, with four limbs and strong gripping claws for crawling along canyon walls.

Gliding

Skin membranes between all four limbs let them glide short distances across canyon gaps.

Posture

They don't stand on two legs well, but can sit back on them to work.

Scales

Rough and spiky, blending into the rock.

Size

115–140 cm long.

Hunters

Their ancestors stayed high up to catch other gliding prey, or to drop onto prey by the river below.

Society & culture

Cliff dwellings

Homes built straight into the canyon walls, like pueblo cliff dwellings on Earth.

Rank by height

The higher you live, the more prestigious. The oldest families live closest to the top.

Shadow & light

Both are worshipped. Shadow keeps them cool and hidden. Light brings life to the canyon floor. The worship the balance  between them.

Water

The river at the bottom of the canyon is precious.
