Keeping time

Makk'ar System

The dates on this wiki

Year zero is the founding of the Kar'avaakro Makrhoto, the Foundation of Knowledge. Each Makk'ar year is one sixth of Oa'Veyra's orbit, and each is named for one of the six major elemental constellations.

SKM
Sovaro Kar'avaakro Makrhoto, “toward the foundation.” Before year zero.
KKM
Kovaro Kar'avaakro Makrhoto, “away from it.” After year zero.

One Makk'ar year is about 48.5 Earth years, and six of them make one full orbit (291 Earth years). Most Makk'ar only live about two and a half “years,” so their calendar has many smaller divisions.

The Makk'ar count in base 6. Every number on this wiki is in base 10.

The Isikitem calendar

The four seasons

Isikitem “eras,” each about 73 Earth years

Each full orbit begins with the Era of Rebirth, so the Isikitem count their years in rebirths. Today is the 42nd. The last season is two days short.
