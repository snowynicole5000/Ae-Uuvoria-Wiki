Planets

The Ae'Uuvoria System

At the center burns Ae'Uuvoria, a blue-white star that is also the creator of everything in the solar system. Its six planets each embody one of the six major elements of magic. Three of them harbor life, and one is home to nearly all the people on this wiki.

The Star

Ae'Uuvoria

THE CORE · “The Primordial Light”

The blue-white star at the center of the system, a Δ Architect, a god. The star and the creator are one and the same. It made all six worlds and set the rules of magic and life within them.

The six worlds
I

Au'Taelis

ORDER · “The still one”

The innermost world, about 19 AU from the star. Au'Taelis is tidally locked, with one side always facing the star.

Explore →
II

Ia'Vorien

LIGHT · “The bright child”

A world of silicon-based life, tilted 38° on its axis, about 39 AU out. Since losing their connection to Oa'Veyra, the creator has turned their attention here.

Explore →
III

Oa'Veyra

NATURE · “The lush”

The home world, about 80 AU out. Its plants grow orange, its rings tilt at 19°, and two moons circle it: a small volcanic one and a larger watery one. A faint shimmering barrier wraps the whole planet, keeping most magic out.

Explore →
IV

Iu'Miruun

WATER · “The deep water”

A world covered by a single global ocean. Microbes tint its atmosphere purple, and a volcanic moon keeps it company.

Explore →
V

Ui'Zhaera

CHAOS · “The storm”

A green gas giant knocked almost onto its side, with an 83° tilt. More than 100 moons orbit it.

Explore →
VI

Eu'Nyhl

DARKNESS · “The long night”

A dark carbon-silicate world on a long, stretched-out orbit that takes about 5,000 years to complete. It has a very low albedo, making it nearly invisible even at its closest orbit.
