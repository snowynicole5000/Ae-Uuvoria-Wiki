Overview

The Au'Uuvori were the first people of Oa'Veyra, created by Ae'Uuvoria. They lived during the Era of Magic, using magic under the creator's rules, and built a civilization whose ruins still lie beneath the world today.

Their end came from within. A faction of Au'Uuvori overloaded the core of magic and locked the creator out, plunging the world into the Era of Darkness. To save what was left, six five of them gave their lives to raise the barrier. Almost all the rest died out. Today only one is known to survive: Eo'Sevoria, the Librarian.

In their language, the peoples who came after are the Ia'Uuvori, “the second born of the divine light.”

Anatomy

Body

Part owl, part bat, part fox.

Feathers

Fine and flat, lying close to the body.

Glow

Their plumage can react to ultraviolet light.

Eyes

Forward-facing, dark amber where the whites would be, with copper-gold irises.

Crest & tail

A decorative crest, and a long bird-of-paradise tail.

Color

Any color at all. No color belongs to any one morph.

Six morphs

Names

  • A birth name is a parent's name plus a prefix for birth order.
  • An adult name is chosen at apprenticeship: Prefix'Word, where the word means something to them.

Sound

  • The language is meant to sound wispy.
  • The apostrophe is only visual. It breaks up long runs of vowels and isn't a pause.
  • They count in base 6, and don't treat zero as a number.
History
~16,800,000 SKM

The first ancients

Long after setting up life on Oa'Veyra, Ae'Uuvoria creates the Au'Uuvori.

The Era of Magic

A golden age

The ancients build across the whole planet with a nearly indestructible material, run their machines on six-state line script, and attempt great experiments, including one that became the Grove. Magic flows freely under the creator's rules.

~16,700,000 SKM

The banishment

A faction overloads the core and locks Ae'Uuvoria out. The creator steps away from the planet, magic runs wild, and the Era of Darkness begins.

~16,699,950 SKM

The end of the ancients

About fifty Makk'ar years later, six Au'Uuvori were supposed to sacrifice themselves to raise the barrier, however only 5 did. The Au'Uuvori almost entirely die out due to the destruction proceeding the banishment.

Today · 5 KKM

The last one

Eo'Sevoria lives on, above the barrier, still in contact with the creator.

What they left behind

The ancient material

Stone-like and nearly indestructible, but not magic itself. Giant rune-lined printers took a little raw feedstock and used Nature and Order nodes to expand, arrange and solidify it, like spray foam. There were printworks all over the world.

The tunnels

Perfectly hexagonal tunnels with no tool marks and no melting, as if the stone had simply been removed from existence.

Writing & technology

Glyph, node and line scripts. Their machines ran on six states instead of two, and line script still glows faintly on ruin walls.

The Sink

An ancient printworks under the Glass City that collapsed into a sinkhole. At the very bottom, a sealed chamber holds the ancient who became Order.

The Grove

A failed experiment. They tried to connect everyone so each could feel the others while staying themselves. It became a true hive mind instead, built around its lead biologist, now only a shell of who they were.

The constellations

The ancients read the star patterns of the sky as writing. Today they're used for enchanting.
