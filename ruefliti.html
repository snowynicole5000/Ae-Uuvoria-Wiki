Overview

The Ruefliti are four-armed amphibians who live in water-level caves beneath the coastal cliffs of the southern continent on Oa'Veyra. They believe water and reflections reveal everything.

They're half right. A reflection really can show whether someone is lying. Reading the future in it, though, doesn't work at all.

Belief & magic

Reflections reveal truth

This one is real. A person's reflection can show whether they're lying.

Magic today →

Reading people

Diviners read a person's reflection to tell their future or their deepest desires. It doesn't work, but it's just vague enough to believe, like tarot or astrology.

Reading the sky

The sky reflected in water, best at night, is read to tell the future of everyone. This doesn't work either.

Origins & home

The Ruefliti come from an amphibious lineage. They slowly specialized for life on land, but kept their strong swimming, their working gills, and their need for moisture.

They live at the north-west tip of the southern continent, in caves at the waterline under the coastal cliffs. They rarely deal with their neighbors, the Ventlings.
