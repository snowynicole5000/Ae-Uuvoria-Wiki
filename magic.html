The Ae'Uuvoria system

Magic

Magic on Oa'Veyra was set up by the creator, Ae'Uuvoria, but it runs mostly on its own. It is built from six major elements arranged in a hexagon, six minor elements between them, and the core at the center that holds it all together.

The cost

All magic costs a piece of the soul. Objects have no soul, so items used to channel magic don't really pay it. Living beings do, and every use takes or corrupts the parts of themselves they cherish most.

Eras of magic
~4 million years~20,700,000 – 16,700,000 SKM

The Era of Magic

The golden age. The Au'Uuvori used magic under the guidance and rules of Ae'Uuvoria, rules meant to protect their people from its heavy cost.

~50 years~16,700,000 – 16,699,950 SKM

The Era of Darkness

A group of Au'Uuvori overloaded the core and locked Ae'Uuvoria out of the system. The lockout was brief, but the creator felt so betrayed that they stepped away from the planet anyway. With magic ungoverned, anyone could use it, creatures included. Heavy use corrupted many of them, and the world fell into a magical apocalypse.

To the present~16,699,950 SKM – now

The Era of Rebirth

In the last years of the apocalypse, six five Au'Uuvori sacrificed themselves to block magic from the planet. Only a few thousand people were left, scattered and broken, and the Au'Uuvori almost entirely died out. The block was never total: light still had to reach the planet for life to restart. Magic today is weak and only reachable through convoluted methods.

Writing magic

The Au'Uuvori wrote magic down in several ways.

Glyph based

Each major element has its own glyph. Combine two neighbors to get the minor element between them.

Node based

Six nodes, one for each major element, around the core. Every other symbol, combination and spell is written by connecting them.

  • Any node can connect to any other.
  • One line must wrap around the core once. Its two nodes are the spell's dominant elements.
  • A line drawn off into nothing, the void, stands for a single node, but makes the spell unstable.
  • Position/rotation matters unlike the other systems. 

Line based

Used mostly in the ancients' technology. Each major element is a different kind of line. Their machines ran on six states instead of binary's two, with each line type as a different function.

Constellation enchanting

The sky is divided into hexagonal regions, one for each element. Carve, paint, or mark an object with a constellation from a region, and it can channel that element. It's used almost entirely by the Makk'ar and the people of the Glass City. The ancients read these star patterns as writing. The Makk'ar see the animals of their pantheon.

Elements only. Constellations channel elements, never spells.

Magic today

Solar refraction

Anyone who can shape glass

Bent and shaped glass can focus light, especially sunlight, into powerful rays that work like laser beams. It can also amplify ordinary light magic.

Spectral separation

A group working with stained glass and prisms

By picking out specific wavelengths of light with stained glass and prisms, they can channel both major and minor elements.

Reflection divination

The peoples of the southern continent

Reflections really can reveal truths. But they also believe the ripples in their divine reflecting pools answer questions about relationships, choices and the future. That part works like tarot or astrology: vague enough to mean something to whoever is listening.

Moon divination

The Isikitem

The Isikitem read the two moons to predict seasons and weather, and worship them as the gods who bring good harvests. It isn't light magic at all. It's science.
