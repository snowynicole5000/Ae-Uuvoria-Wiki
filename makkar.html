Overview

The Makk'ar are omnivorous forest dwellers from the north of the western continent on Oa'Veyra. They are well into the iron age, and still expanding.

They founded the Kar'avaakro Makrhoto, the Foundation of Knowledge. Its founding is year zero of the calendar used on this wiki, and their counting of years in SKM and KKM comes from it.

Their reach goes far beyond their forests. The Glass City began as a Makk'ar colony, the Fulgari live under their rule, and they have their eyes on the metal-rich mountains of the Bovidians.

Society & culture

The pantheon

Six animals, each governing one of the six major elements. Where the ancients read the stars as writing, the Makk'ar see the animals of their pantheon.

Constellation enchanting

Carving a constellation onto an object lets it channel that element. It's used almost entirely by the Makk'ar and the people of the Glass City.

Craft & technology

Mostly wood, but well into the iron age. Their academy is researching steam power.

Keeping time

Each Makk'ar year is one sixth of Oa'Veyra's orbit, about 48.5 Earth years, named for one of the six elemental constellations. They count in base 6.

Land & government

The Makk'ar live mostly in the north of the western continent, between its northern tip and the mountains. Most settlements sit along rivers, for trade and for floating logs down to build with.

The main government is a council of the region's wealthiest families. The head of each family holds its seat. Below the council, regional governments of representatives look after each region.

They see the rest of the continent as theirs to take.

Besides the council, notable groups include the academy, a trading guild, and the Makk'ar branch of the delvers' guild.

The Foundation of Knowledge

The Kar'avaakro Makrhoto, the Makk'ar academy, and its five departments.

Materials Study

Began looking for better materials for weapons and lenses. Recently given a huge grant to reverse engineer the ancients' building material.

Astronomy

Searches the stars for new constellations to enchant with. A smaller group studies how to apply them.

Biology

Mostly farming. Added as the population grew and trade with the Isikitem picked up.

History & Linguistics

Not just the ancients' languages and history, though all of it is told the Makk'ar way.

Mechanics

Engineering and steam power, with a small section trying to reverse engineer ancient technology.

The Glass City

A Makk'ar colony on the south coast that grew into a massive trading hub, between the Fulgari desert to the north and the Isikitem lands to the west. The full map and every district →

Who runs it

A council of young heirs from the ruling families, sent to prove themselves. An advisor can veto anything big. The Garrison, the Varrhaku, keeps order.

The glass ladder

Neighborhoods are named after the glass their people can afford: silvered mirror glass for the richest, then clear, green, leaded panes, broken scrap, and furnace slag outside the wall.

Appearance is everything

Fashion, jewelry, pets and building materials all signal class. A thriving underground economy keeps the bottom running, and the council pretends it doesn't exist.

The Sink

A sinkhole in the poor east of the city, once an ancient printworks. Shacks line its rim, a black market fills the old halls, and ruins lie at the bottom.
