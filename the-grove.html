Overview

The Grove is an aspen-like forest on the south-west coast of the western continent, all connected by a single fungal network. It is one organism, spread across a whole region.

It began as an experiment by the Au'Uuvori. They tried to connect everyone, so each person could feel and speak with all the others while staying themselves. It failed. The network became a true hive mind instead, with the experiment's lead biologist at its core. After so much time, and so much absorbed into it, the biologist is now only a shell of who they were.

How it lives

Body

An aspen-like forest joined by one fungal network.

Spread

The fungus can infect other plants and animals, which then tend to and protect the Grove.

Members

Not one species. Anything infected becomes part of it, mostly local wildlife.

Signs

Infected creatures show fungal growth on their bodies. The more infected, the more it shows.

Reach

Members are only truly controlled within range of its spores. They can leave, but the infected can still feel the urge to go back.

Manner

Avoids large contact and tries to stay unknown. It takes in small groups of travelers instead.

The worshippers

A group that worships the Grove, made up mostly of Isikitem, Ruefliti, Bovidians, Tassik, and possibly Ilsaru.

Partly infected

Members are only partly infected at first so they can tend the Grove and help it grow.

The elders

The most infected members can “hear” the hive, so they are the spiritual leaders. Everyone else exists to serve and protect the Grove.

Becoming one

Being fully consumed isn't death to them. It means becoming one with the hive, the most revered act of faith there is.
