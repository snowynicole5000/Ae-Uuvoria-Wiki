Overview

The Isikitem are small, tusked farmers from the grasslands of the western continent on Oa'Veyra, west of the Glass City. They worship the planet's two moons as sisters and ask them for good harvests.

Their calendar, built around the moons, is used for everyday life across the western continent, and by the Ruefliti in the south. How it works →

Anatomy

Body

Four limbs. They run on all fours, but stand upright for most tasks.

Hands

Four fingers each: three main digits with two segments, and one minor digit with one. Eight fingers in all, so they count in base 8.

Tusks

Two large tusks jutting down from the sides of their face. Once for digging up truffle-like food, now for plowing and harvesting.

Ears

Large, straight or floppy. Their hearing is excellent.

Eyes

Set wide for broad awareness, left over from their ancestors being prey.

Fur

Soft, in earthy tones that match the grassland and forest edges.

The sister moons

Both moons are worshipped. They're used to predict the weather and the seasons, which is science, not magic.

Atathetem

/ӓtaθɛtɛm/, the younger sister. The close, fast moon. Small, joyful prayers greet her return, and mournful ones mark her disappearance.

Society & culture

Farming

Herbivores who first dug up truffle-like food with their tusks. As farming developed, the tusks became their tools.

Craft

Sewing, weaving, and loom work.

Technology

Early to mid copper age. They know how to forge metal, but rarely do, since there's so little of it where they're from.

Villages & towns

Mostly small villages and farms. A few central towns serve as hubs where the larger ceremonies happen and village and family leaders gather to make big decisions.

North & south

Northern Isikitem

Larger, more organized collectives. They trade with the Glass City and use the railway it built, and hold a trade seat on its council.

Southern & interior Isikitem

Traditionalist and purely agricultural, and suspicious of outside influence.
