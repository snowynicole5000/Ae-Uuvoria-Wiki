Bovidians

Horned stone carvers of the eastern peaks
Homeland
Northern mountains of the east

Lifespan

Overview

The Bovidians are huge, shaggy herbivores from one of the tallest mountain ranges on Oa'Veyra, in the north of the eastern continent. They live mostly underground, in halls built almost entirely from stone, and they are master stone carvers.

Their mountains are rich in metal, better than anything the Makk'ar have, and the Makk'ar want it.

Anatomy

Body

Shaped like a giant ground sloth: massive and heavy-set. They walk on all fours, or rear up to use their hands.

Size

250–300 cm standing upright, built for climbing and digging in steep, rocky ground.

Head

Goat-like, with large spiraling horns that never stop growing.

Fur

The warmest of any species, from life high in the mountains. Usually white, cream or light gray.

Senses

Horizontal, goat-like pupils and strong hearing. They can feel vibrations through stone.

Diet

Herbivores. They graze the mountain slopes and farm hardy root crops.

Society & culture

Underground

Most Bovidians live below ground, in structures built almost entirely of stone.

Stone carving

Very skilled carvers.

Horns

Since horns never stop growing, their size shows age. Decorations added through life mark life events.

The halls

Each hall is like an anthill, and mostly governs itself. Ironvein Hall is one of them.

Relations

The Makk'ar

The Makk'ar want Bovidian territory for its metal and are trying to start a war. They invited an envoy from Ironvein Hall, then killed the envoy on the way back to their rooms.
