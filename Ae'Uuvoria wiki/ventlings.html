Overview

The Ventlings are snail-like extremophiles with shells of iridescent bismuth, scattered across the volcanic regions of the southern continent on Oa'Veyra. They are the oldest civilization of the current era.

They aren't cruel, but they never evolved to be social. They have little interest in anyone, including each other.

Anatomy

Body

Snail-like, 40–60 cm.

Shell

Iridescent bismuth, inspired by the real scaly-foot snail and its iron shell.

Habitat

Extremophiles that thrive in heat and volcanic conditions.

Lifespan

About 240 Earth years, the longest of any people today.

History
The Era of Darkness

Survivors

Their tiny, non-sapient ancestors live through the mass extinction.

The Era of Rebirth

Becoming a people

With almost no competition, they slowly develop into a basic sapient group, the first civilization of the new era.

Today · 5 KKM

Apart

Scattered and few, and irrelevant to world politics.

Society & culture

Low empathy

Not cruel, just not social. They only come together to raise families, or now and then to build something bigger that helps themselves.

Distrust of light

An instinctive distrust of light and magic, inherited from ancestors who survived the Era of Darkness.

Bismuth

They don't make art on purpose, but their bismuth creations are highly valued in the Glass City. They get there through indirect trade, since Ventlings don't care for outsiders.
