Planet III  ·  Nature

Oa'Veyra

“The lush” — the home world  ·  oh-ah-VAY-rah

Oa'Veyra is the third world from Ae'Uuvoria and the planet of Nature. Nearly all the people on this wiki live here, spread across its continents and oceans.

Life here looks different from Earth's. Its plants grow orange, so on Oa'Veyra orange is the color associated with life instead of green. Its days are long, its air is thick, and its gravity is 1.5 times that of Earth's.

A ring circles the planet, tilted away from its equator, and two moons cross its sky. The Isikitem see them as sisters and build their whole calendar around them.

Around the entire planet hangs the barrier, a faint, aurora-like shimmer that blocks almost all magic from reaching the surface.

Moons

Moon A

Atathetem, “the younger sister” to the Isikitem

The small, close moon. Volcanic, glowing faintly at night. Its quick orbit sets the 8-day Isikitem week. Its return is greeted with small joyful prayers, and its disappearance is mourned.

  • Orbit  8 days (~12.8 Earth days)
  • Distance  ~271,000 km
  • Radius  ~1,200 km  ·  Gravity  0.18 g
  • Radius  ~1,200 km

Moon B

Etathetem, “the older sister” to the Isikitem

The larger, watery blue moon on a slow, distant orbit. It is so much farther away that it looks smaller in the sky than Moon A. Each of its orbits is one Isikitem “knot,” and its return is celebrated with a great festival.

  • Orbit  64 days (~102 Earth days)
  • Distance  ~1,082,000 km
  • Radius  ~1,800 km  ·  Gravity  0.31 g
  • Radius  ~1,800 km
The barrier

At the end of the Era of Darkness, six five Au'Uuvori gave their lives to raise a barrier around Oa'Veyra. From the ground it shows as a faint, aurora-like shimmer across the sky.

Beneath it, most elemental magic is unusable. Only very faint light magic still gets through.

Only one place breaks through it: the peak of the tallest mountain, which pierces the barrier entirely.
