Overview

The Ilsaru are desert reptiles from the eastern continent of Oa'Veyra, with scales that shift color and eyes that see finer shades than any other species. Color is everything to them.

Their stained glass is both art and magic. Their artisans and their priests are one and the same.

Anatomy

Body

Two arms and two legs.

Tail

Very long: 90–100 cm.

Scales

Shift color with their mood. It began as camouflage and heat control, and became a way to talk.

Eyes

Two main eyes with color vision that sees far finer shades than any other species.

Pit eyes

Five more along the spine, sensing basic movement.

Lifespan

About 110 Earth years.

Society & culture

Spectral separation

Stained glass and prisms split light into specific wavelengths, and each can channel a different element.
