Planet IV  ·  Water

Iu'Miruun

“The deep water”  ·  ee-oo-mih-ROON

Iu'Miruun is the fourth planet from Ae'Uuvoria and the world of Water. A single ocean covers the entire planet. There is no land anywhere.

It sits just past the outer edge of the star's habitable zone, but a thick, CO₂-heavy atmosphere traps enough heat to keep its ocean warm, around 30–35 °C. Microbes floating in that atmosphere tint its clouds purple-green.

Its ocean is full of life, carbon-based like Oa'Veyra's, including creatures on a leviathan scale.

Iu'Miruun's only moon

A close, volcanic moon with an iron-silicate interior and a sulfur-covered surface, a lot like Jupiter's moon Io. It glows faintly, and it hangs large in Iu'Miruun's sky, about three times the size of Earth's Moon as seen from Earth.
